## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Computer Science Engineer, Research Engineer and Researcher working at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Data Science and Cybersecurity.

My academic background combines two engineering degrees in Computer Science, with specializations in Computer Networks and IT Maintenance, and Software Analysis and Programming, obtained at the École Supérieure d'Informatique (ESI), Université Nazi BONI, Burkina Faso.

This engineering background provides me with a broad foundation in software design and development, computer systems, networking, cloud computing, IT infrastructure, databases and cybersecurity.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Luxembourg, where I conducted doctoral and postdoctoral research within the TruX Research Group at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT).

My doctoral research was funded by the Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR) through the AFR Bilateral project [BURGEON](https://www.uni.lu/snt-en/research-projects/burgeon/), in collaboration with Singapore Management University (SMU).

My work combines applied research, AI engineering, software development, experimental evaluation and the development of reproducible research tools.

My professional experience also includes IT consulting, cloud infrastructure, systems and network administration, professional training, higher education, educational technology and academic programme coordination.

## 🚀 Selected Research Projects

Research software, tools, datasets, benchmarks and replication packages developed as part of my research activities.

| **Project** | **Research Focus & Contributions** | **Resources** |
|---|---|---|
| **TestHumanizer** | LLM-based refactoring of automatically generated unit tests, with automated validation, readability and maintainability assessment. | [Repository](https://github.com/Cwendkuuni/TestHumanizer) |
| **Desc2Fix** | Semantic alignment between bug reports, software tests and patches using structured information extraction, LLMs and human evaluation. | [Repository](https://github.com/Cwendkuuni/Desc2Fix) |
| **CoReEval** | Large-scale benchmarking of LLMs as code readability evaluators against human judgements. | [Repository](https://github.com/Cwendkuuni/CoReEval) |
| **LLMTSDiff** | Large-scale empirical analysis of test smells in LLM-generated, search-based and human-written unit tests. | [Repository](https://github.com/Cwendkuuni/LLMTSDiff) |
| **CCTR** | Cognitive complexity measurement for unit test code, incorporating structural and test-specific constructs. | [Repository](https://github.com/Cwendkuuni/CCTR) |
| **CTSES** | Composite evaluation metric for assessing structural and semantic preservation in LLM-based test refactoring. | [Repository](https://github.com/Cwendkuuni/CTSES) |
| **Bot4UnitTesting** | Interactive AI-assisted unit test generation and software testing automation. | [Repository](https://github.com/Cwendkuuni/Bot4UnitTesting) |

Additional research software and replication packages will be released as they become publicly available.
